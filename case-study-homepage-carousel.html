Customizable homepage carousel

Design + front-end build · Enterprise marketing site

Problem

Marketing needed to customize homepage carousel slides by target audience without engineering involvement each time.

Constraints

Multi-region
Content needed translation support across regions
Third-party A/B testing
Component had to integrate with an external test platform

Approach

Built a slide component with configurable audience targeting, structured so copy and imagery stayed swappable per locale and per A/B variant served by the third-party platform.

Result

Delivered a customizable widget, built on the third-party platform, where a marketer's custom slide overrides the existing homepage carousel.

Reflection

Once marketing had the tool in hand, they ran with it — using the flexibility to create compelling, creative slides beyond what I'd initially anticipated.
